Zen Guard

Take control of your browsing. Stay focused.

Add to Chrome

Zen Windows

Create time-based blocking schedules with custom start and end times for each day of the week. Block distractions only when you need to focus.

Global Rules

Set rules that apply at all times, regardless of your active Zen Window. Perfect for sites you never want to visit.

Flexible Blocking

Block by exact URL, domain, or keywords found in URLs, page headings, and content. Even block specific YouTube channels.

Custom Messages

Display your own motivational messages when a page is blocked. Remind yourself why you're staying focused.

Track Your Progress

See weekly stats on how many distractions were blocked. Celebrate your focus wins.

Import & Export

Backup and restore your configuration. Share your blocking rules across devices.

How It Works

  1. Create a Zen Window with your focus hours (e.g., 9am-5pm on weekdays)
  2. Add rules to block distracting sites like social media and news
  3. Stay focused - when you try to visit a blocked site, you'll see your custom message
  4. Use Global Rules for sites you want blocked at all times
